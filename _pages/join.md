**Currently recruiting Ph.D. students and research assistants.** *(Last updated:
July 2026)*
{: .notice--success}

I am building a research group at Saint Louis University on **natural language
processing for event-centric narrative understanding** — studying how events
become narratives and building transparent systems for comparing those narratives
across sources, languages, and media. You do not need experience in every area;
useful backgrounds include NLP/ML, linguistics, data science, computer vision,
visualization, and computational social science.

## Prospective Ph.D. Students

Apply to the [SLU Computer Science Ph.D. program](https://www.slu.edu/science-and-engineering/academics/graduate-programs/computer-science-phd.php#text)
and name *Jin Zhao* and your research interests in your statement. You are also
welcome to email me a short note with your interests, why my group is a fit, your
CV, and (optionally) a representative paper, thesis, or repository. Subject line:
**Prospective Ph.D. Student – [term] – [your name]**.

## Undergraduate & Master's Research Assistants

SLU undergraduate and master's students looking for research experience in NLP/AI
are welcome to reach out. Projects range from annotating event and framing
relations, to evaluating LLMs, to building datasets and visualization tools. Prior
research experience helps but is not required; Python, ML/NLP, web development, or
languages other than English are all useful.

Email me a short note with your program and year, relevant skills, interests, and
availability. Subject line: **Research Assistant – [term] – [your name]**.

**Contact:** jin.zhao@slu.edu
