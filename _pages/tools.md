Tools, datasets, and resources from my research. Where a resource is released with
a paper, follow the paper link for details.

## Software

- **UMR Writer** — a web application for annotating Uniform Meaning
  Representations. [Tool](http://umr-tool.cs.brandeis.edu/) ·
  [Paper (EMNLP 2021 Demo)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-demo.19) ·
  [UMR-Writer 2.0 (LAW-XVII 2023)](https://aclanthology.org/2023.law-1.21)
- **Chinese PropBank Lookup** — a lookup tool for the Chinese PropBank.
  [Tool](https://chinese-propbank.herokuapp.com/)
